Fun facts

Fun fact 1

It's a pipe inside a pipe

It's the simplest heat exchanger there is. One fluid runs through the inner pipe, and the other flows through the gap around it, called the annulus.

Fun fact 2

Hairpins save space

Bending the pipes into U-shaped "hairpins" fits a long flow path into a small frame, like the U-bends on this unit.

Fun fact 3

True counterflow

It's one of the few exchangers that gets pure countercurrent flow, so its LMTD design needs no correction factor.

Fun facts

Fun fact 4

The temperature cross

In counterflow, the cold fluid can leave hotter than the hot fluid leaves. In cocurrent flow, that can't happen.

Fun fact 5

Built for pressure

Small-diameter pipes hold high pressures well, and the parts come apart easily for cleaning.

Fun fact 6

Wrapped for a reason

The foil insulation on this unit keeps heat from escaping into the air, so more of it goes into the other fluid.
